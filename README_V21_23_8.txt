World Drive V21.23.8 — Tractor look rework

Changes vs V21.23.7:
- reworked tractor wheels to stay clearly larger than the original truck, but with more believable proportions;
- front and rear tractor wheels now use different realistic visual sizes;
- reworked front fenders / wheel arches to integrate better with the cab and avoid the blocky look;
- enlarged / repositioned black side windows on the doors for better visibility.

No changes to truck camera, torque, steering, suspension or terrain.
