WORLD DRIVE V21.21.11 — VALIDATION / QA

STATUS
Candidate. Automated/static QA passed; real driving feel remains to be validated by the user.

ROOT CAUSE
V21.21.10 produced rearLateral ~= 1.0 under handbrake cornering, so the friction circle itself was detecting rear saturation correctly. However, the lost rear lateral force never entered the chassis yaw dynamics. Only a small historical rear-slip yaw heuristic remained, which was far too weak to create an obvious handbrake rotation.

FIX
Inside the existing 20 Hz per-wheel grip solver:
1. Compute remaining lateral capacity from longitudinal friction-circle demand.
2. Compute the lateral force scale available at each wheel.
3. Compare that force to the balanced bicycle-model force already assumed by the steering model.
4. Sum the resulting force-loss moment around the CG.
5. Convert moment to angular acceleration with the vehicle yaw inertia.
6. Integrate that angular acceleration into dynamicYawRate every rendered frame.

No direct handbrake-to-yaw constant is used. With zero signed lateral demand, the computed yaw moment is exactly zero.

DEDICATED HAND BRAKE QA
PASS
- WRX, straight handbrake: friction yaw acceleration = 0.
- WRX, coasting corner without longitudinal saturation: extra friction yaw = 0.
- Left/right sign symmetry: PASS.
- All 7 current vehicles create correctly signed yaw acceleration during a moderate handbrake corner.
- WRX at 4.0 m/s^2 lateral demand: 3.020 rad/s^2 friction-derived yaw acceleration.
- Deterministic 60 FPS maneuver, 72 km/h, 0.5 s handbrake:
  - +18.06 degrees chassis heading versus the same turn without handbrake.
  - 6.05 degrees maximum chassis/velocity sideslip.
  - 46.48 deg/s maximum yaw rate.
- Same maneuver in a straight line: no chassis rotation / no sideslip.
