World Drive V21.24.4 — Countach cockpit + rear lights

Countach GLB improvements
- Capot / first-person mode is now overridden by a true seated driver camera inside the authored Countach cabin.
- Driver eye is positioned on the left-hand steering-wheel side at realistic low Countach head height.
- First-person head-look can rotate freely within realistic limits.
  - gamepad: right stick
  - mouse: hold/drag left button directly on the 3D view
- camera near plane is reduced only while in the Countach cockpit to limit interior clipping.
- brake input illuminates bright red rear brake lamps.
- reverse engagement / backward motion illuminates white rear reverse lamps.
- the real GLB wheels remain the visible wheels; procedural wheel pivots stay hidden and physics-only.

Unchanged
- Countach physics, grip, steering, suspension and audio
- V21.22.6 terrain / satellite ownership / hitch-free streaming
- both truck profiles and all other passenger vehicles
