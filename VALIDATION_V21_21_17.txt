Sideways landing stress test
----------------------------
Initial state: WRX 120 km/h, chassis 35 degrees across its momentum vector.
After 0.75 s on the ground:
- coasting: 22.88 degrees of sideslip still remains
- full brake: 30.57 degrees of sideslip still remains, speed 91.4 km/h

This is intentional: braking consumes the same friction budget needed to bend
linear momentum. Braking may decelerate and rotate the chassis, but it cannot
create supernatural lateral grip.
