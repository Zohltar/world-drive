V21.21.27 stable baseline.

Scope
-----
Distant-terrain rendering only. Vehicle physics/presentation are unchanged.

Implemented
-----------
- Dedicated distant-terrain colour pass; no 14 m major/minor contour bands.
- Altitude/slope vegetation-to-rock palette with low-frequency world variation.
- Progressive distance desaturation before normal Three.js scene fog.
- Far-only elevation smoothing; exact near seam and road corridors protected.
- Horizon rows: 13 -> 15, same 5860 m outer half-extent (~8.3 km corners).
- Horizon material keeps MeshStandardMaterial, fog and day/night lighting;
  dithering enabled.
