Original V21.21.15 reproduction
-------------------------------
The original model reproduced the user's observation under a realistic full
throttle WRX request. From a straight state with maximum RIGHT steering after
1 second:
  80 km/h: chassis -10.88° (wrong sign)
 100 km/h: chassis -14.26° (wrong sign)
 120 km/h: chassis -16.87° (wrong sign)
 150 km/h: chassis -21.79° (wrong sign)
LEFT was exactly symmetric in the opposite wrong direction.

This confirms the fault was in the force/yaw coupling, not in the user's input.
