Historical note
V21_21_PHYSICS_QA.mjs contains a deliberate hard-coded assertion that the fleet
size is exactly seven and that every profile has exactly two axles. It therefore
fails its obsolete fleet-shape assertion once V21.23.0 correctly adds an eighth,
three-axle tractor. Its seven-car behavioral coverage is superseded here by the
passing fixed-ID V21.21.26 calibration/traction suites plus the new V21.23.0 QA.
