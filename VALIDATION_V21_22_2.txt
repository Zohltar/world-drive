WORLD DRIVE V21.22.2 — NEAR-QUALITY MEDIUM TERRAIN CANDIDATE

STATUS
Development candidate. Stable baseline remains V21.21.27.

USER-REPORTED ISSUE
V21.22.1 improved the very distant terrain, but the medium-distance band still
looked visibly inferior as soon as the route started. Densifying the horizon
rings reduced facets but did not remove the fundamental visual hand-off.

CHANGE OF STRATEGY
V21.22.2 no longer asks the procedural horizon to imitate near terrain in the
critical medium band. Instead, the actual high-detail ground mesh and imagery
footprint are expanded:
- 3200 m / 256 segments -> 5600 m / 448 segments
- exact grid spacing remains 12.5 m
- high-detail half extent grows from 1600 m to 2800 m
- procedural horizon starts only after 2800 m
- route-ahead DEM + imagery prefetch extends to 5 km

PRESERVED
- V21.22.0 distant colour/haze pass
- V21.22.1 32-row dense far-horizon ladder
- V21.21.27 road rendering and streaming architecture
- all V21.21.26 vehicle calibration and physics

EXPECTED COST
- high-detail ground: 448 x 448 cells ~= 401k triangles (previously ~=131k)
- rebuild cost and imagery coverage are intentionally higher
- no additional per-frame vehicle physics cost
