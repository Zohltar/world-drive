World Drive V21.21.22 — Load Hotfix Validation

User-reported V21.21.21 runtime error:
  ReferenceError: Cannot access 'speedAbs' before initialization
  at updateDrive (main.js:5454)

Root cause:
V21.21.21 added speed-dependent aerodynamic load to longitudinal traction calls.
Those calls referenced `speedAbs`, but the existing `const speedAbs=Math.abs(speed)`
was intentionally declared later in updateDrive(), after longitudinal speed integration,
for steering/lateral physics. JavaScript therefore hit the temporal dead zone on frame 1.

Fix:
- Capture `const longitudinalSpeedAbs=Math.abs(speed)` before longitudinal traction calls.
- Pass that value to drive, service-brake and handbrake traction calculations.
- Keep the later post-integration `speedAbs` unchanged for steering/lateral physics.
