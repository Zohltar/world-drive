World Drive V21.21.23 — F1 Aero Stability Validation

Goal
- Make the 2010 F1 substantially harder to slide than road cars.
- Prevent the F1 from becoming airborne too easily over road crests at speed.
- Preserve road-car physics, graphics recovery, right-lane assist, braking momentum and prior performance work.

F1 calibration
- Mass: 740 kg (unchanged from V21.21.22 representative running mass).
- ClA: 4.56 m² (unchanged).
- Aero grip efficiency: 0.88 (was 0.84).
- Lateral acceleration base limit: 20.5 m/s² (was 18.5).
- Front tire grip scale: 1.20.
- Rear tire grip scale: 1.20.
- Power-oversteer helper reduced: grip loss 0.018, yaw 0.010.
- Crest retention: 100% of theoretical downforce contributes to vertical support before takeoff.
- Airborne retained aero: 55% of theoretical wing downforce continues to accelerate the car downward after takeoff.

Measured F1 envelope
- 100 km/h: ~2.64 g lateral envelope.
- 200 km/h: ~4.28 g lateral envelope.
- 300 km/h: ~6.27 g lateral envelope (3.0x aero-grip cap).
- 200 km/h downforce: ~8.62 kN (~879 kg equivalent).
- 200 km/h supported downward authority: ~21.46 m/s² (gravity + aero).
- 200 km/h airborne downward authority: ~16.22 m/s² (gravity + 55% retained aero).
- At 100 km/h and 12 m/s² lateral acceleration (~1.22 g), max normalized F1 tire usage is ~0.484 with no slip.
- At 100 km/h, F1 lateral envelope is >2.7x the WRX envelope in the same model.

Notes
- Road cars have no aeroDownforceClA, so the new vertical-aero path contributes exactly zero to them.
- Tire aero normal load remains disabled while airborne; the retained airborne wing force is only applied to vertical chassis motion, not magically converted into tire grip.
- No artificial 'F1 = glued to road' position correction was added. Takeoff remains possible when a crest requires more downward acceleration than gravity + available aerodynamic load.
- No full Vite/Electron build was run because this candidate package does not include node_modules.
