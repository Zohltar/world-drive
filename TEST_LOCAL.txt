   selection cannot make terrain remain above the visible road.

5. Transition-over-road fuse
   Each terrain triangle checks all 3 vertices, all 3 edge midpoints and its centroid.
   If any sample is inside the protected road corridor of any branch, that triangle is not drawn.

   range, instead of shading the artificial cut surface itself.
