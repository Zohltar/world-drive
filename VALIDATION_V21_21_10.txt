FRICTION CIRCLE
The previous model computed combined tire stress but rear handling slip was based only on lateral demand. V21.21.10 computes remaining lateral capacity from normalized longitudinal demand:
  lateralCapacity = sqrt(max(0, 1 - longitudinalUtil^2))
Then effective lateral utilization is evaluated against that remaining capacity.
A small numerical floor avoids division instability for a fully locked wheel.

EXPECTED RESULT
- corner + handbrake -> rear-dominant lateral saturation / drift initiation
- straight + handbrake -> longitudinal lock/stress but zero artificial lateral-slip demand
- combined service brake + corner -> less lateral reserve than cornering alone

PERFORMANCE
The new sqrt/divide executes only in the secondary per-wheel grip solver (20 Hz). Node synthetic microbenchmark measured ~0.581 us/call before vs ~0.618 us/call after (~+6.5% inside that tiny solver, ~0.037 us absolute per call). This should be negligible relative to a rendered frame, but it is not a browser FPS guarantee.
