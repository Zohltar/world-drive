World Drive V21.24.5 — Countach authored rear lights

Changes vs V21.24.4
- Removed the four synthetic rear-light box overlays added in V21.24.4.
- Brake and reverse illumination now use the actual lamp geometry already present in the SINNIK Countach GLB.
- The GLB SignalLights rear cluster is the dynamic red brake-light material.
- The GLB Lights rear cluster is the dynamic white reverse-light material.
- Front portions of those meshes remain on their original GLB material; only rear-fascia triangles are assigned the dynamic material.
- Cockpit camera and first-person head-look from V21.24.4 are preserved.
- Countach physics, suspension probes, audio, terrain and other vehicles are unchanged.
